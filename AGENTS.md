# Persona / Project Guidelines

- **Target Audience:** This application is for the user's **personal use only**. Do not optimize for scale, multi-tenancy, or other users.
- **Data Persistence:** The user's saved data (like personas, uploaded files, etc.) is highly important. NEVER delete or overwrite saved files, local databases, or storage without explicit instruction from the user. Preserve their state across edits.
- **Development Focus:** Optimize for the user's specific preferences, workflow, and local ease of use.
